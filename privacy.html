Privacy Policy

Corridor · last updated 14 August 2026

Corridor collects nothing. There is no server, no account, no sign-in and no analytics. Nothing you do in the app is sent anywhere, and there is nowhere for it to be sent to.

Your location

The app asks for your location so it can show where you are on the trail, measure how far you have walked, and warn you about water sources ahead. That location is read on the phone, used on the phone, and stored on the phone. It is never transmitted, never logged off the device, and never shared with anyone — including me.

You can refuse the permission or withdraw it at any time in iOS Settings. The app still works; it simply cannot draw where you are.

What is stored on your phone

  • Your journey — the days you walked, where you slept, your destination.
  • Your preferences — which trail, which map layers, which direction.
  • Any route you drew yourself.
  • The downloaded map for each trail.

All of it lives inside the app's own storage and is deleted when you delete the app.

Network use

The app itself makes no network requests. The one thing that crosses the network is the map for a trail, which you choose to download: iOS fetches it from Apple's own servers using Apple's Background Assets framework. I do not operate that server and I receive no information about who downloads what.

Third parties

There are none. No advertising, no tracking, no crash reporting, no third-party SDKs of any kind.

Children

Because nothing is collected, nothing is collected from children either.

Changes
