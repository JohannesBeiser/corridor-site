The real app, on the Great Divide Trail, around Wolverine Pass.

What it answers

  • How far to the next water, the next place to sleep, the next town.
  • What's worth knowing before the next section — permits, river crossings, seasonal closures, long water carries.
  • How far you've walked today and what's left to where you're stopping.
  • What an alternate costs you, before you commit to it.

It works with no signal at all

A trail's whole topographic map downloads once and works with the phone in airplane mode. No signal, no server, no account. Contours, hillshading, ground cover, huts, water, towns and the roads you walk in on.

A note on the data

Every waypoint comes from the trail's own association or from open data. I can't hand-verify all of them, and no water source is truly reliable until someone who was there says so. If you're out and something's wrong, tell me and I'll fix it.

Corridor is a planning and navigation aid, not a substitute for a paper map, a compass, or the trail organisation's own current notices.

— Pez, who walked the CDT, the PCT, the GDT and Te Araroa, and built this because he wanted one app that answered the questions he actually had out there.
